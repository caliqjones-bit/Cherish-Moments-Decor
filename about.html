About Cherish Moments

Designing Holiday Experiences That Become Lasting Traditions

Who We Are

A Luxury Holiday Tree Design Studio

At Cherish Moments, we believe the holidays deserve more than beautiful decorations—they deserve thoughtful design, intentional detail, and spaces that bring people together.

We are a luxury holiday tree design studio specializing in custom seasonal installations for residential and corporate clients. Every project is uniquely designed to complement the architecture, interior aesthetic, and personality of the space, creating a holiday experience that is both timeless and unforgettable.

Our philosophy is simple: luxury is found in the details.

From Concept to Completion

Every Detail, Thoughtfully Managed

From the first consultation to the final ornament, we thoughtfully manage every aspect of the design process. We source premium décor, develop a cohesive design concept, professionally install each display, and return after the season for careful takedown and organization.

Our clients enjoy the beauty and magic of the holidays while we handle every detail with care and precision.

Whether transforming a private residence, enhancing a corporate office, styling a hotel lobby, decorating a museum, elevating a wedding venue, or creating a festive showroom, our goal remains the same—to design holiday environments that reflect your vision while creating meaningful moments for those who gather there.

Why It Matters

The Backdrop for Your Memories

At Cherish Moments, we understand that a beautifully designed holiday space is more than décor. It becomes the backdrop for family traditions, celebrations with friends, and memories that are treasured for years to come.

We are honored to be trusted with those moments and are committed to delivering exceptional craftsmanship, personalized service, and a seamless experience from concept to completion.
