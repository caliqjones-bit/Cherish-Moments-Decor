How Pricing Works

What Shapes Your Proposal

Each project is customized based on the following:

Tree Height

Taller trees require more materials and installation time.

Number of Trees

Single centerpiece or coordinated multi-tree installations.

Design Complexity

Layering density, custom elements, and bespoke details.

Installation Requirements

Access, ceiling height, and site conditions.

Property Size & Areas

Number of decorated areas across the property.

Décor Sourcing

New premium décor versus your existing inventory.

Residential or Corporate Scope

Scale, coordination, and scheduling needs.

Takedown & Organization

Post-season removal and storage preparation.

All projects begin with a consultation. A customized proposal will be provided after the design needs, tree height, décor requirements, installation conditions, and project scope have been reviewed.
