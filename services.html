The White-Glove Holiday Experience

From the first conversation to the final ornament — and back again after the season — every detail is designed, sourced, installed, and cared for by one dedicated team.

Personalized Design Consultation

A private consultation to understand your space, style, goals, color preferences, existing décor, budget, timeline, and vision. This conversation shapes everything that follows.

Custom Holiday Tree Design

A completely curated holiday tree concept tailored to the architecture, interior design, and personality of the space — from palette and ribbon work to ornament layering and lighting.

Best suited for: homeowners and businesses who want a signature centerpiece designed exclusively for their space.

Design My Tree

Premium Décor Sourcing

Professional sourcing of ornaments, ribbon, lighting, florals, picks, tree toppers, garlands, and coordinating decorative elements — selected for quality, cohesion, and longevity.

Start With a Consultation

Professional Installation & Styling

Complete installation performed with precision, balance, safety, and meticulous attention to detail. Every ribbon curl, pick, and ornament is placed by hand.

Best suited for: clients who want a flawless result without lifting a single ornament box.

Seasonal Décor Coordination

A cohesive decorative plan that connects trees, mantels, staircases, entryways, garlands, wreaths, and surrounding rooms into one considered composition.

Best suited for: whole-home installations and multi-area commercial properties.

Plan My Season

Garland, Wreath, Mantel, Staircase & Entryway Styling

Custom seasonal styling for the architectural features and focal areas of your property — the moments guests notice first and remember longest.

Style My Space

Post-Season Takedown & Organization

Careful removal, sorting, and organization of holiday décor after the season — so next year begins beautifully, too.
