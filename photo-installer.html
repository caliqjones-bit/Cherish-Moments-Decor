Photo Installer

Owner tool for Cherish Moments Decor. Use this page once to place your real photos into the website, then delete it (do not upload it to your web host).

How it works — 3 steps:
  1. Click “Connect the photo folder” below and choose the assets → img folder inside your cherish-moments-decor website folder. (Chrome or Edge required for automatic saving.)
  2. For each card below, click “Choose this photo…” and pick the matching picture from your computer. Each card describes exactly which photo it wants.
  3. That's it — the photo is automatically renamed, resized for the web, and saved in the right place. The card turns green when done.
