Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I reserve my holiday installation?

As early as possible — our installation calendar fills quickly ahead of the season. Reserving early also gives us the most time for design development and décor sourcing. Owner note: add a specific booking window when confirmed

Do you provide the ornaments and décor?

Yes. Premium décor sourcing is one of our core services — we professionally source ornaments, ribbon, lighting, florals, picks, toppers, and garlands for your project. Décor materials are quoted separately from the starting design and installation price.

Can you work with decorations I already own?

Absolutely. We are happy to incorporate your existing décor and family pieces into the design, supplementing with sourced elements where needed for a cohesive result.

Do you decorate both residential and corporate spaces?

Yes — we serve private residences as well as corporate offices, hotel lobbies, museums, wedding venues, automotive showrooms, retail locations, restaurants, and other commercial spaces.

Is post-season takedown available?

Yes. We return after the season for careful removal, sorting, and organization of your holiday décor.

What areas of Central Florida do you serve?

We serve clients throughout Central Florida. Contact us with your location and we will confirm availability for your area.

How is my final project price determined?

Your customized proposal is based on tree height, number of trees, design complexity, installation requirements, property size, number of decorated areas, décor sourcing needs, any existing inventory, and whether takedown is included. Standard Tree Design starts at $1,000 and Luxury Tree Design starts at $2,500 — décor materials are not included in starting prices.

Do you decorate more than Christmas trees?

Yes — garlands, wreaths, mantels, staircases, entryways, tablescapes, and full-room seasonal styling are all part of our work.

Can you coordinate multiple trees and rooms?

Yes. Whole-home and multi-area commercial installations are a specialty — we create one cohesive design across every decorated space.

What happens after I book a consultation?

Your request is reviewed by our team, who will contact you to confirm your date and time. Before we meet, please complete the Design Questionnaire so we can prepare tailored ideas. After the consultation, you receive a customized proposal for your project.
